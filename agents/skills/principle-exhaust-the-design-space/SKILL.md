# Exhaust the design space

Explore distinct approaches when important requirements or user preferences leave the design uncertain. Use the smallest sketches, prototypes, or experiments that expose the tradeoffs.

Choose the number and depth of alternatives for the decision. A second option can be enough. Follow an established pattern or a single viable approach when the constraints already determine the answer.

Compare against concrete requirements and observed behavior. Ask the user to choose when the remaining difference is a preference. Do not turn a mechanical edit or a bug with a clear target into a design exercise.
