# Document modes

Choose the structure for the reader's task. Use distinct sections or linked documents when different purposes need substantial detail.

- A tutorial teaches through a sequence the reader can complete. State what they will build, supply the needed context, and give an observable result at each important step.
- A how-to guide solves a task for someone who already understands the domain. Give actions, prerequisites, relevant alternatives, and expected results. Link background material when needed.
- A reference provides facts for lookup. Mirror the structure of the thing described and cover options, limits, and errors. Distinguish established facts from information that is not known.
- An explanation develops understanding. Explain the mechanism, constraints, history, and consequential tradeoffs at the depth the question needs.

Do not force a document into one mode when a combined structure serves the reader better. Keep transitions clear and omit sections that do not help.
